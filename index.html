About

Melinda Carr

BSN, RN, CPN

Melinda Carr is the primary consultant and owner of Carr Legal Nurse Consulting.

She has over 39 years of experience in ICU, Critical Care Transport, and Procedural Sedation with an emphasis on Pediatrics. She holds a certification in Pediatrics and historically has held certifications in Neonatal Intensive Care and a certificate in Trauma.

Her professional passion has been caring and advocating for her patients. As a Legal Nurse Consultant she brings her experience and knowledge of medical records, medical/nursing dynamics, and clinical scenarios to your legal team — whether plaintiff or defendant. As a member of the American Association of Legal Nurse Consultants, she has a multitude of resources to support your legal team.

What We Do

Services

Comprehensive legal nurse consulting from case screening through trial support.

Case Merit Analysis

Analyze cases to determine viability and identify strengths before litigation.

Medical Records Review

Expert review and analysis of medical records, including investigation for complete records and documents.

Pain & Suffering Reports

Detailed pain and suffering reports and fact witness services to support your case.

Medical Cost Projections

Assist with projecting future medical costs accurately for your client's needs.

Timeline Preparation

Precise chronological organization of medical events to support narrative clarity at trial.

Audit Trail Analysis

Motions to compel for production, analysis and complete investigative reports.

Expert Witness Locating

Identify and vet qualified medical expert witnesses suited to your specific case needs.

Personal Injury Assessment

Assessment of the nature, extent, and permanency of damages — including future medical problems and potential expenses.
